MetaboLab · Privacy Policy

Your health information deserves a quiet place.

This Privacy Policy explains what MetaboLab collects, why we use it, when it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

Effective date: September 23, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 23, 2026  ·  Policy version: 2026-09-23-v2

In brief: MetaboLab does not require an account or ask for your name to use the core app. Cloud sync is off until you enable it. Optional AI features identify OpenAI by name and describe the data before a separate in-app confirmation; the free report preview is generated on your device. We do not sell health information or use it for advertising.

This Privacy Policy applies to the MetaboLab mobile application and related services (the “Service”). “MetaboLab,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the operator of the Service. This policy does not apply to Apple, RevenueCat, Cloudflare, OpenAI, your laboratory, your healthcare provider, or other third parties when they process information under their own policies.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to provide

  • Profile context: age or birth year, biological sex if provided, height, and weight.
  • Health and lab information: A1C, glucose readings, fasting context, fasting insulin, triglycerides, other supported lipid or metabolic markers, units, reference ranges, dates, notes, and related trends.
  • Reports and files: photos, scans, PDFs, or manually entered values that you choose to import. Original images or PDFs are processed for extraction on the device or during the import flow; our current cloud synchronization stores structured values and not the original lab image or PDF.
  • Focus information: selected areas of focus, completion state, review or retest dates, and reminders.
  • Support information: your email address and the contents of a message if you contact us.

B. Information created automatically

  • Installation identifier: a random device-bound installation ID used to associate your app data with this installation. It is not your name or email address.
  • Security material: a public key and request-signing metadata used to authenticate the installation and protect API requests. The corresponding private key is kept in the device Keychain.
  • Subscription information: product, entitlement, transaction, renewal, and purchase status information supplied by Apple or our subscription-management provider. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details.
  • Technical information: app version, platform, request timestamps, basic diagnostic information, and security or error logs needed to operate and protect the Service. We do not use the app for cross-app advertising tracking.

3. Where information comes from

Information may come directly from you, from your device during app operation, from Apple when you make or restore a purchase, from RevenueCat or a similar entitlement service, or from service providers that help us run the Service. We do not obtain your laboratory records, insurance records, or medical records from a healthcare provider unless a future feature clearly asks you to authorize that connection.

4. How we use information

We use information only for purposes such as:

  • providing the app’s tracking, import, review, trend, snapshot, and focus features;
  • saving and synchronizing the structured data you ask us to synchronize;
  • checking data formats, calculating non-clinical comparisons, and presenting educational context;
  • generating an optional AI summary or focus plan when you request the feature and are eligible to use it;
  • processing purchases, checking entitlements, restoring purchases, and preventing purchase abuse;
  • responding to support requests and communicating about the Service;
  • monitoring reliability, troubleshooting errors, protecting security, preventing misuse, and complying with law; and
  • improving the Service using aggregated or de-identified information where reasonably possible.

We do not sell your health information. We do not use health, lab, or profile information to serve targeted advertising, to build advertising profiles, or to make insurance, employment, credit, or similar eligibility decisions.

5. AI summaries and automated processing

Recipient and route: optional AI summaries are processed by the OpenAI API (OpenAI) through MetaboLab’s Cloudflare Workers service. The first time you enable summaries—or after you withdraw consent or the disclosure changes—the app shows OpenAI by name, the data categories, and retention information. We send data only after you accept that disclosure and request “Generate” or “Refresh” analysis. The free report preview is calculated on your device; the remote preview endpoint is disabled in the current Worker configuration.

Summary data sent: confirmed current report values and marker names, units, dates, report source, fasting context, available lab ranges and explanations; up to six prior reports; up to 50 glucose readings with dates and context; selected profile details (age or birth year, height, weight, and goal); focus-plan details and completion state; and, if you connected Apple Health, a bounded summary of selected data from up to seven days (such as average steps, sleep, active days, and selected weight or glucose context). Your name, email address, original report photo or PDF, and raw OCR text are not included in the summary request. We limit and normalize the fields again on the Worker before forwarding them.

Optional report-image extraction: local on-device reading is tried first. If it cannot map supported values, the app may offer enhanced extraction. Before every image upload, a separate screen names OpenAI and describes the transfer. Only if you tap “Send images to OpenAI” are up to four selected report pages resized on your device and sent through our Cloudflare Worker to OpenAI for one-time value extraction. Pages may contain identifiers such as your name, birth date, or record number; crop or cover details you do not want to share. The original photo or PDF remains on your device. The Worker processes the resized upload to fulfil the request and does not write image bytes to MetaboLab’s D1 database or application storage. Choosing another import method does not send the images.

Our OpenAI API requests set store:false. OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models by default. This setting does not disable provider safety or abuse-monitoring logs: OpenAI says standard abuse-monitoring logs may contain prompts and responses and may retain them for up to 30 days; different controls may apply to eligible accounts. See OpenAI API data controls. OpenAI processes submitted information to return a structured educational summary or extract values. AI output can be wrong or incomplete and is not medical advice.

Do not include names, addresses, medical record numbers, or another person’s information in notes or files. The Worker does not intentionally log full health request or response bodies and does not write AI request data to MetaboLab’s D1 health-data tables.

6. When information may be shared

We share information only as needed for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Cloudflare: hosts our API on Workers and structured app data on D1 when you enable synchronization; it also transmits optional AI requests to OpenAI.
  • OpenAI: receives the specific structured health/profile fields in Section 5 only after you accept the in-app AI disclosure and request an analysis. It receives selected report-page images only after the separate, one-time image-upload confirmation.
  • Apple: app distribution, device services, Apple Health authorization, and in-app purchase processing. Apple may receive transaction and device information under Apple’s privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat: subscription entitlement management, renewals, restores, and purchase events. It receives installation or purchase identifiers and entitlement status, not your full payment-card number.
  • Support and professional advisers: when needed to answer your request, protect rights, or comply with legal obligations.
  • Authorities or other parties: when required by law, legal process, fraud prevention, security, or to protect users and the public.

Each provider processes information under the terms and privacy documentation applicable to the service and account configuration we use. Those terms, retention periods, safeguards, and processing locations can differ by provider; see Section 5 and the linked provider documentation for the information we have verified. We configure our use to send only the information needed for the requested feature. We do not share health information with advertisers, data brokers, or social networks for advertising or cross-service tracking. We do not share it with a clinician unless you choose to export or share a report.

7. Where and how long we keep information

Health and profile information is stored locally on your device. Cloud synchronization is off by default; if you enable it, structured information may be stored in our Cloudflare D1 database and associated with your random installation identifier. The backend does not store original report images or PDFs. We do not require an account for the core experience, so reinstalling the app or changing devices may not identify the same installation unless the product provides a supported restore path.

We keep synchronized information for as long as needed to provide the Service, honor your requests, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, maintain security, and meet legal or accounting obligations. You can request deletion of the associated active D1 records from the app’s “Delete health data” control. Provider safety logs, encrypted backups, security logs, or records required by law may persist for a limited period under the applicable retention schedule. Turning sync off stops future synchronization but does not itself remove an existing cloud copy.

You should export or save anything you need before deleting the app or requesting deletion. Deleting the app alone may not immediately delete server-side synchronized data or cancel an Apple subscription.

No app, network, storage system, or transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee that unauthorized access, loss, or alteration will never occur.

Protect your device passcode and do not share exported reports or screenshots publicly. Tell us promptly at matt1232026@gmail.com if you believe your data or account-related purchase information has been misused.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, MetaboLab requests read-only access to selected categories shown on Apple's permission screen, such as steps and activity, sleep, weight, and blood glucose when available. You can allow or deny each category separately.

MetaboLab uses this information only to provide health-management features such as personalized educational context, plans, and reminders. We do not use Apple Health information for advertising, sell it, or use it for data mining. You can change Apple Health permissions at any time in the Health app or your device Settings. You can also request deletion of synchronized MetaboLab data using the process in Section 9.
