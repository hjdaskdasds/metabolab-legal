MetaboLab · Privacy Policy

Your health information deserves a quiet place.

This Privacy Policy explains what MetaboLab collects, why we use it, when it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

Effective date: September 1, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 1, 2026

In brief: MetaboLab does not require an account or ask for your name to use the core app. We collect the information needed to provide device-bound synchronization, subscriptions, support, and optional AI summaries. We do not sell health information or use it for advertising.

1. Scope and who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the MetaboLab mobile application and related services (the “Service”). “MetaboLab,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the operator of the Service. This policy does not apply to Apple, RevenueCat, Cloudflare, OpenAI, your laboratory, your healthcare provider, or other third parties when they process information under their own policies.

For privacy questions or requests, contact matt1232026@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you choose to provide

  • Profile context: age or birth year, biological sex if provided, height, and weight.
  • Health and lab information: A1C, glucose readings, fasting context, fasting insulin, triglycerides, other supported lipid or metabolic markers, units, reference ranges, dates, notes, and related trends.
  • Reports and files: photos, scans, PDFs, or manually entered values that you choose to import. Original images or PDFs are processed for extraction on the device or during the import flow; our current cloud synchronization stores structured values and not the original lab image or PDF.
  • Focus information: selected areas of focus, completion state, review or retest dates, and reminders.
  • Support information: your email address and the contents of a message if you contact us.

B. Information created automatically

  • Installation identifier: a random device-bound installation ID used to associate your app data with this installation. It is not your name or email address.
  • Security material: a public key and request-signing metadata used to authenticate the installation and protect API requests. The corresponding private key is kept in the device Keychain.
  • Subscription information: product, entitlement, transaction, renewal, and purchase status information supplied by Apple or our subscription-management provider. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details.
  • Technical information: app version, platform, request timestamps, basic diagnostic information, and security or error logs needed to operate and protect the Service. We do not use the app for cross-app advertising tracking.

3. Where information comes from

Information may come directly from you, from your device during app operation, from Apple when you make or restore a purchase, from RevenueCat or a similar entitlement service, or from service providers that help us run the Service. We do not obtain your laboratory records, insurance records, or medical records from a healthcare provider unless a future feature clearly asks you to authorize that connection.

4. How we use information

We use information only for purposes such as:

  • providing the app’s tracking, import, review, trend, snapshot, and focus features;
  • saving and synchronizing the structured data you ask us to synchronize;
  • checking data formats, calculating non-clinical comparisons, and presenting educational context;
  • generating an optional AI summary or focus plan when you request the feature and are eligible to use it;
  • processing purchases, checking entitlements, restoring purchases, and preventing purchase abuse;
  • responding to support requests and communicating about the Service;
  • monitoring reliability, troubleshooting errors, protecting security, preventing misuse, and complying with law; and
  • improving the Service using aggregated or de-identified information where reasonably possible.

We do not sell your health information. We do not use health, lab, or profile information to serve targeted advertising, to build advertising profiles, or to make insurance, employment, credit, or similar eligibility decisions.

5. AI summaries and automated processing

If you request an AI summary, MetaboLab sends only the structured, confirmed information needed for that summary, such as marker names, values, units, dates, fasting context, and selected profile context where relevant. The current summary flow is designed not to send the original lab photo, PDF, or raw OCR text to the AI endpoint.

The AI provider processes that information to return a structured educational summary, markers to review, focus areas, questions for a clinician, and a review plan. AI output can be wrong or incomplete and is not medical advice. Do not include names, addresses, medical record numbers, or other information about another person in notes or files.

We may use limited technical logs to diagnose failed requests, but we do not intentionally log the full health payload in ordinary application logs. Where a third-party provider’s retention or processing terms apply, that provider may process information as described in its own privacy documentation.

6. When information may be shared

We may disclose information to the following categories of recipients, only as reasonably necessary:

  • Apple: app distribution, device services, and in-app purchase processing. Apple may receive transaction and device information under Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Cloud infrastructure providers: hosting, API delivery, database storage, security, and synchronization. The current backend uses Cloudflare Workers and D1 for structured app data.
  • Subscription providers: RevenueCat or a similar provider to validate products, entitlements, renewals, restores, and subscription events. These providers receive identifiers and purchase status, not your full payment-card number.
  • AI providers: only when you request an AI feature, using the structured data described in Section 5.
  • Support and professional advisers: when needed to answer your request, protect rights, or comply with legal obligations, subject to appropriate confidentiality requirements.
  • Authorities or other parties: when required by law, legal process, fraud prevention, security, or to protect users and the public.

We do not share health information with advertisers, data brokers, or social networks for advertising or cross-service tracking. We do not share your information with a clinician unless you choose to export or share a report from the app.

7. Where and how long we keep information

Some information is stored locally on your device. If synchronization is enabled or required for a feature, structured data may be stored on our backend and associated with your random installation identifier. We do not require a user account for the core experience, so reinstalling the app or changing devices may not identify the same installation unless the product explicitly provides a supported restore path.

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the Service, honor your requests, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, maintain security, and meet legal or accounting obligations. When you request deletion, we aim to delete associated structured app data from active systems within a reasonable period. Encrypted backups, security logs, or records required by law may persist for a limited period before routine deletion or irreversible de-identification.

You should export or save anything you need before deleting the app or requesting deletion. Deleting the app alone may not immediately delete server-side synchronized data or cancel an Apple subscription.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the information, including encrypted transport, device Keychain storage for sensitive installation credentials, and device-bound request signing for backend requests. No app, network, storage system, or transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee that unauthorized access, loss, or alteration will never occur.

Protect your device passcode and do not share exported reports or screenshots publicly. Tell us promptly at matt1232026@gmail.com if you believe your data or account-related purchase information has been misused.

9. Your choices and privacy requests

  • Provide or skip: profile fields and report entry are optional for the core exploration experience. You can choose what to add.
  • Correct: review and edit values in the app before confirming a report, and correct saved information through the available edit controls.
  • Delete: request deletion of synchronized app data by emailing us. Include the installation identifier shown in the app if available; do not email more health information than needed.
  • Withdraw AI use: do not request the AI summary feature. Previously generated summaries may remain in the app or synchronized data until deleted.
  • Subscriptions: manage, cancel, or request a refund for Apple purchases through the Apple Account and Apple’s support processes. We cannot cancel an Apple subscription on your behalf.
  • Legal rights: depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal information, and to complain to a data-protection authority. We will verify and respond to requests as required by applicable law.
